About PBSA Logistics

Who We Are

PBSA Logistics is a professional logistics provider operating in the private and commercial sectors. With over 15 years of experience, we have built a reputation for safety, reliability, and excellence. Our fleet of 50+ vehicles serves clients across South Africa.

We are Level 1 B-BBEE contributor and RTMS accredited, ensuring the highest standards of service and compliance.
